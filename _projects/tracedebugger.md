A lightweight back-in-time debugger to trace and retrace past method invocations and state changes in Squeak.
It also offers a [query tool](https://dl.acm.org/doi/epdf/10.1145/3594671.3594678) that lets you visualize how objects grow over time.
Powered by the [SimulationStudio](https://github.com/LinqLover/SimulationStudio) framework, which explorers the idea of modifying code interpretation without changing every method or the virtual machine.
